Three things were wrong and all three are fixed together, because they share a cause — only the LinkedIn button was ever brought up to standard and the other two were left as the vendored theme wrote them. 1. `{{ page.title }}` was interpolated raw into a query string, and `url_encode` appears nowhere else in this repo either. A title containing & truncated the tweet at that point and injected a bogus parameter; # dropped everything after it. Titles on this site routinely contain both : and (), and now that posts carry their real descriptive names they are longer and likelier to. 2. Twitter and Facebook were icon-only links whose sole child was an empty Font Awesome , so they had no accessible name at all — a screen reader announced the share block as three links, one of them called "Share on LinkedIn" and two called "link". aria-label names them; aria-hidden on the stops the icon font being read as text. 3. target="_blank" without rel="noopener" hands the opened page a window.opener reference back to this one. The onclick popups are kept: they degrade to a normal navigation when JS is off, and `return false` only suppresses the default when the popup actually opened. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign share_url = page.url | absolute_url | url_encode -%} {%- assign share_title = page.title | strip_html | url_encode -%}
